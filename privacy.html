Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026.

Spire is a private app for its owner's personal use. It runs on the owner's own computer and is not offered to anyone else.

Google account data

With the owner's consent, the app reads and sends mail from the owner's own Gmail accounts. It asks for no other Google data.

Where the data goes

Data is stored on the owner's computer. When the owner asks the app's AI assistant for help, the text it needs is sent to the AI provider to answer. Data is not sold or used for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at Google account permissions.
